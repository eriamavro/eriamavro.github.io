About

Senior Compositor

Based in London, UK, I am a Senior Compositor with over 25 years in the wider screen industry, including more than 20 years in visual effects. My work spans Hollywood feature films, streaming series, commercials, immersive projects, and game cinematics.

I specialize in high-quality compositing, primarily using Foundry Nuke, and have collaborated with teams across the globe in fast-paced production environments.

I am also a Full Member of BAFTA (British Academy of Film and Television Arts), serving as a 2024 Television Craft Awards judge in the Titles & Graphic Identity category. This reflects my long-standing dedication to the craft of visual storytelling.

Independent UK–Japan education and outreach initiatives, supporting engagement with programmes such as BAFTA, particularly in youth and student-focused contexts.
